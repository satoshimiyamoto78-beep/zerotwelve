What does “Order this package” mean?

You get the same scope, specs and deliverables as the example, adapted to your brand, at the listed price. We confirm details on a 20-minute call before you pay anything.

And “Order something similar”?

The example becomes a reference. We change the scope to fit your goal and send a fixed quote within three days.

How are prices calculated?

Starting price comes from the format and the approach. Extra versions, AI voice-over, original music and rush delivery are added on top. The estimator shows each line.

Won’t AI video look cheap?

AI without direction does. We storyboard, light and compose AI scenes like a film, then grade and mix them. A frame that wouldn’t pass as real doesn’t make the cut.

Can you edit footage we already have?

Yes, from $400. We edit, grade, mix and cut versions for every platform. Missing shots are generated to match.

Do you work internationally?

Yes. Everything happens online: calls, reviews and files through a secure cloud. You send footage, we send films.
